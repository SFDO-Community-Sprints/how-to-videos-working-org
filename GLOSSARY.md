# How-To Videos

The language of the Nonprofit How-To Series: how people ask for how-to content, how the team chooses what to make, and how a piece of content moves from an idea to publication. Terms follow the group's charter where the charter names them.

## Language

### Requests and triage

**Request**:
One person's ask for a piece of how-to content, or their report of a problem with existing content.
_Avoid_: Case (except as the Salesforce object name), ticket, submission, idea (for this meaning)

**Requester**:
The person who made a request, known by the name and email address they gave.
_Avoid_: Submitter, customer, contact, user

**Triage**:
The check each new request gets: whether it is in scope, and whether content already exists for it.
_Avoid_: Review, screening, moderation

**Qualify**:
The triage outcome that ties a request to a content record, new or existing. A request that is out of scope is not qualified, and the requester is told why.
_Avoid_: Accept (for this meaning), approve, close

**Triage Queue**:
The list of new requests that wait for triage.
_Avoid_: Queue (alone), inbox, backlog

### Content

**Content**:
One piece of how-to material that the team makes, from first idea to the published result.
_Avoid_: Video (inside the team's workspace), project, content item

**Idea**:
Content that the team has not yet accepted or declined. Its status is Submitted.
_Avoid_: Request (for this meaning), suggestion, proposal

**Status**:
Where content is in its life: Submitted, Accepted, Drafting, Proofing, Production, Editing, Final Review, Published, Retired, or Declined.
_Avoid_: Stage, phase, step

**Champion**:
The identified person who stands behind an idea. By default the champion is the requester whose request created the content.
_Avoid_: Submitter, author, owner

**Accept**:
Team leadership's unanimous decision, made at a convening, that an idea will be made. Accepted content joins the priority queue.
_Avoid_: Qualify (for this meaning), approve, convert

**Decline**:
Team leadership's decision that an idea will not be made, always with a team response.
_Avoid_: Reject, close, archive

**Team Response**:
The team's reply on an idea that it declines.
_Avoid_: Comment, note, reason

**Holder**:
The volunteer who has custody of content at its current status.
_Avoid_: Owner, assignee, custodian

**Artifact**:
One of the three recordings that the Production status must produce: the scratch track, the screen recording, and the production track.
_Avoid_: Asset, deliverable, file

**Retire**:
To withdraw published content because it is outdated or wrong.
_Avoid_: Archive, unpublish, delete

### Demand

**Request Count**:
The number of different requesters whose requests point to one content record.
_Avoid_: Votes (for this meaning), duplicates, hits

**Vote**:
One signed-in person's support for one idea. A person has at most one vote on an idea and can withdraw it.
_Avoid_: Like, point, upvote (as a noun), request (for this meaning)

### Prioritization

**Priority Queue**:
The single ordered list of accepted content to produce next. It has no tiers, and it changes only at a convening.
_Avoid_: Backlog, production queue, queue (alone, because Salesforce uses "queue" for a group that owns records)

**Convening**:
A Commons sprint, in person or virtual, or an official team meeting between sprints. Decisions about the priority queue and the charter are made only here.
_Avoid_: Event, meeting (alone), venue

### People

**Team Leadership**:
The durable members of the team who run sprints, accept and decline ideas, and make key decisions.
_Avoid_: Admins, moderators

**Queue Manager**:
The one person at a time who triages requests and manages the priority queue: keeps content moving, sees that work is assigned, and clears roadblocks.
_Avoid_: Project manager, coordinator, triager

**Production Manager**:
A team member trained and equipped to join the production track to the recording, and who approves the final edit.
_Avoid_: Editor, producer

**Drafter**:
The volunteer who writes the script for a piece of content.
_Avoid_: Writer, author

**Proofer**:
The volunteer who reviews a script against the team's standards. The charter calls this role QA.
_Avoid_: Reviewer, QA (alone)

**Member**:
A person who signs in to the public site with their Trailblazer ID. Members arrive in a later round.
_Avoid_: User, voter, customer, external user

**Visitor**:
A person who uses the public site without signing in.
_Avoid_: Guest, anonymous user
